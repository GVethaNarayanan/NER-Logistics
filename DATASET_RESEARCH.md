\# Dataset Research \& Verification



\## 1. Purpose



This document records the actual research, verification and selection of datasets and APIs that will be used to build the AI-Based Smart Logistics \& Accessibility Intelligence Platform.



The objective is to identify reliable, accessible and legally usable data for the NER region.



\---



\## 2. Research Objectives



The research will identify usable data for:



\- Historical transportation disruptions

\- Weather conditions

\- Road networks

\- Bridges and infrastructure

\- Traffic conditions

\- Geographic and routing information

\- Field incidents

\- Vehicle/GPS tracking

\---



\## 13. Final Data Strategy



After verification, the project will use the most reliable combination of:



\- Open/public data

\- Verified government data where accessible

\- Weather APIs

\- Platform-generated field reports

\- Simulated GPS data for V1 where real GPS is unavailable



No fabricated real-world data will be presented as genuine.
