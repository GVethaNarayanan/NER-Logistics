\# Project Scope



\## Problem Statement



Transportation across the North Eastern Region can be affected by difficult terrain, unpredictable weather, road and bridge disruptions, and limited connectivity. These challenges can cause delays in the movement of essential goods and make timely logistics decisions difficult.



\## Proposed Solution



The proposed system is an AI-powered logistics intelligence platform that monitors transportation conditions, predicts route disruption and accessibility risks, recommends safer alternatives, tracks logistics vehicles and provides alerts through a centralized GIS-based dashboard.



\## V1 Scope



The first version will focus on:



\- Monitoring road and bridge conditions

\- Integrating weather information

\- Predicting route disruption/accessibility risk

\- Recommending alternative routes

\- Tracking logistics vehicles

\- Recording field incidents

\- Generating alerts

\- Visualizing logistics information through GIS



\## Future Scope



The system can later be extended with RAG, AI agents, computer vision, IoT sensors, satellite data, offline support and multi-agent decision-making.
